# Candidates — judged by the local Crown judge (round-robin, 10 games)

## Winner: v1 (179 characters)

> "Imagine every post office, library, and market in the world joined by one road, where any letter you send arrives before the ink dries and any book answers when you call its name."

**Why v1 won:** the single comparison (one road joining every post office, library, and market) carries the whole explanation, it uses only things an 1800 traveler already knows, it is true of the internet read literally, every word earns its place in 179 characters, and it leaves two sticky images — a letter arriving before the ink dries, and a book that answers when called by name. The runner-up v2 loses the market and the "road" image, and its "in a blink" is a weaker, less memorable picture than "before the ink dries".
